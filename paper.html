Final Survey Paper

Agent-Based Modeling and Simulation of Urban Traffic

Planned

Content updated: November 8, 2026

Paper Overview

The final paper will examine how agent-based models represent travel decisions and traffic controls to study congestion. It will compare behavioral assumptions, simulation methods, computational requirements, and evidence supporting congestion-management strategies.

All 60 publications in the bibliography will be referenced in relevant discussions throughout the paper. The 20 papers selected for thorough reading will provide the main detailed comparisons, while the wider literature will support the background, related work, and discussion of research trends.

New York City will remain the motivating case. A possible NYC traffic model and its connection to DEVSJAVA will be discussed as future work.

Planned Structure

  1. Abstract, problem introduction, and motivation.
  2. Basic terminology, background, and related work.
  3. Literature selection and the research classification scheme.
  4. Major approaches and representative methods within each category, including major results that justify the classification and discussion of supporting and minor results.
  5. Comparative findings, supporting evidence, and limitations.
  6. Research trends, recommendations, and future work.
  7. Conclusions and references covering all 60 bibliography publications.

Manuscript Availability

The manuscript is not yet available. Its abstract and PDF will be added here when a draft is ready to share. The final submission is due December 10, 2026.
