Presentation Overview

This 35-slide presentation is designed for 30 minutes of speaking followed by 5 minutes of questions. It draws on our 60-publication bibliography, with detailed comparisons centered on the 20-paper shortlist.

The slides explain the classification scheme, modeling and computational approaches, reported results, and limits of the available evidence. They also discuss congestion management and a proposed future NYC corridor model.
